Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 12, 2026

Notice Regarding Google AdSense: MarketPulse partners with third-party advertising vendors, including Google LLC, to display contextual advertisements. Google uses cookies to serve ads based on user visits to this and other websites across the Internet.

1. Introduction

MarketPulse Media LLC ("MarketPulse", "we", "us", or "our") is dedicated to protecting your personal data and privacy. This Privacy Policy details our procedures regarding the collection, storage, use, and disclosure of information when you access our stock market news platform, financial tools, and application programming interfaces.

2. Google AdSense & DoubleClick DART Cookies

Google is one of our primary third-party vendors. Google uses cookies, specifically the DoubleClick DART cookie, to serve advertisements to visitors of MarketPulse based on their prior browsing history on our website and other websites on the Internet.

  • Third-party ad servers or ad networks use technology in their respective advertisements and links that appear on MarketPulse, which are sent directly to users' browsers.
  • They automatically receive your IP address when this occurs. Other technologies (such as cookies, JavaScript, or Web Beacons) may also be used to measure advertising effectiveness and personalize ad content.
  • Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, users can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

3. Information We Collect

We collect information in two main ways:

  • Information You Voluntarily Provide: Email address when subscribing to our market morning brief or submitting messages via our contact form.
  • Automatically Collected Telemetry: Log data including your Internet Protocol (IP) address, browser type, Internet Service Provider (ISP), date/time stamps, referring/exit pages, and click counts to analyze trends and administer the site.

4. GDPR Compliance (European Economic Area)

If you are a resident of the European Economic Area (EEA), you possess statutory data protection rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including:

  • The right to access, rectify, or erase the personal information we hold about you.
  • The right to restrict or object to the processing of your data.
  • The right to data portability.

5. CCPA/CPRA Privacy Rights (California Residents)

Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California consumers have the right to request that we disclose categories of personal data collected, request deletion, and opt-out of the sale or sharing of personal data. MarketPulse does not sell consumer personal data.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out through our Contact Page or by emailing privacy@marketpulse.live.
